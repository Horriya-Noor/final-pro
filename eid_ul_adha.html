Spirit of Sacrifice
عيد القربان

The noble sacrifice of Prophet Ibrahim (AS) reminds us that true devotion means giving what we love most for the sake of Allah. May your heart always be filled with that beautiful faith and surrender.

"وَمَن يُسْلِمْ وَجْهَهُ إِلَى اللَّهِ وَهُوَ مُحْسِنٌ فَقَدِ اسْتَمْسَكَ بِالْعُرْوَةِ الْوُثْقَىٰ"
